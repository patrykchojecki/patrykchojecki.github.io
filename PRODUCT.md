# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The site serves a broad audience rather than one narrowly defined segment. Visitors may include founders, investors and partners, potential employers, professional peers, friends, and people who discover Patryk through his work or interests. Their primary job is to learn who Patryk is—professionally and personally—and understand what he works on, cares about, builds, and invests in.

## Product Purpose

`chojecki.net` is Patryk Chojecki’s independent home on the web. It brings together his current work, career history, founder support, investment interests, projects, personal interests, and current activities in one place.

Success means a visitor leaves with a rounded and recognizably personal understanding of Patryk that a conventional résumé or social profile would not provide. The site should make it easy to explore further or connect with him without reducing the experience to a single conversion goal.

## Positioning

This is a first-person personal site rather than a generic professional profile. Its value comes from combining Patryk’s work in startup accelerators and venture capital with his founder-support experience, personal investing interests, projects, tastes, observations, and life in Taiwan. The site should reveal more of Patryk’s perspective and personality than LinkedIn or a résumé can.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the public website at `chojecki.net` through a set of complementary surfaces:

- the home/about page introduces Patryk and his journey;
- the CV records professional experience, education, and languages;
- the projects archive documents hobby projects and experiments;
- the `/now` page shares current activities and interests;
- contact and social links provide optional ways to continue the relationship.

The site is maintained as a Jekyll project, deployed to GitHub Pages, and owned independently through the existing custom domain.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the current position at 886 Studios and keep time-sensitive career facts accurate.
- Preserve founder support as a central part of Patryk’s professional identity.
- Represent investment interests across both Patryk’s professional work and personal life.
- Maintain the CV and project archive as substantive parts of the site.
- Keep the public site compatible with the existing Jekyll and GitHub Pages deployment workflow unless Patryk explicitly approves a platform change.
- Do not narrow the whole product toward one audience or one conversion action without explicit direction.
- Do not invent employers, investments, outcomes, testimonials, credentials, or quantitative claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Patryk Chojecki.
- Domain: `chojecki.net`.
- Voice: personal, first-person, candid, and recognizably Patryk rather than generic professional marketing copy.
- Identity: retain the cherry motif as a recognizable personal signature.
- Content balance: professional work and credibility should coexist with interests, taste, projects, investing, and other personal insights.

## Evidence on Hand

- Biography and personal journey: `_pages/about.md`.
- Professional experience, education, languages, and quantified career claims: `_pages/cv.md` and `assets/Patryk Chojecki - CV.pdf`.
- Hobby-project archive and supporting images: `_pages/portfolio.html`, `_portfolio/`, and `images/projects/`.
- Current-activity format and Xbox activity integration: `_pages/now.md`, `_includes/xbox-activity.html`, and `_data/xbox_preview.yml`.
- Identity, contact information, professional links, and site metadata: `_config.yml`, `_data/navigation.yml`, `_data/author_profile_links.yml`, and `images/`.
- Existing writing and personal interests: `_posts/` and `_drafts/`.
- No testimonials, customer logos, press claims, or public investment portfolio were confirmed during initialization; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the whole person. Professional credibility, personal interests, investing, projects, and current life should reinforce one another.
2. Sound like Patryk. Prefer specific first-person observations and real details over résumé boilerplate or personal-brand clichés.
3. Let visitors explore. Support multiple paths through the site instead of forcing everyone into one funnel.
4. Keep facts trustworthy. Career, investment, and project claims must remain current, attributable, and free of invention.
5. Protect independence. Preserve the custom domain, durable content archive, and owner-controlled publishing workflow.
